  desorption         1.93          |  1.93

C. COAL PLANT (660 MW supercritical unit, PLF 80%)
  Net output: 660 MW -> 485 MW  (loss 26.5%)
    steam extraction 99 MW, CO2 compression 58 MW, auxiliaries 18 MW
  Net efficiency (GCV): 35.8% -> 26.3%  (penalty 9.5 points)
  CO2 captured: 3.85 Mt/yr; emission intensity 0.926 -> 0.126 t/MWh
  Capture capex: Rs 5436 crore (Rs 82362 per kW of reference net capacity)
  Electricity cost (LCOE): Rs 4.85 -> Rs 9.30 per kWh  (+Rs 4.45, +92%)
  Cost per tonne CO2 CAPTURED: Rs 3439 (USD 39.1)
      capex                    Rs 1554
      fixed om                 Rs 564
      solvent and chemicals    Rs 270
      energy penalty           Rs 1051
  Cost per tonne CO2 AVOIDED:  Rs 5567 (USD 63.3)

D. CEMENT PLANT (1.98 Mt clinker/yr, 2.83 Mt cement/yr)
  Kiln CO2: 0.805 t/t clinker, 1.59 Mt/yr; steam boiler adds 0.90 Mt/yr (also captured)
  CO2 captured 2.24 Mt/yr; net CO2 avoided 1.13 Mt/yr (71% of kiln emission)
  Steam boiler 333 MWth; electricity 37.7 MW (133 kWh/t CO2)
  Capture capex: Rs 3350 crore
  Cost per tonne CO2 CAPTURED: Rs 5054 (USD 57.4)
      capex                    Rs 1645
      fixed om                 Rs 597
      solvent and chemicals    Rs 270
      electricity              Rs 932
      steam fuel               Rs 1609
  Cost per tonne CO2 AVOIDED:  Rs 10038 (USD 114.1)
  Added cost: Rs 5726 per t clinker, Rs 4008 per t cement (Rs 200 per 50 kg bag)

E. COMPARISON WITH LITERATURE RANGES
  Reboiler duty 3.62 GJ/t vs 3.5-4.0: inside literature range
  Efficiency penalty 9.5 pts vs 8-12: inside literature range
  Compression 105 kWh/t vs 90-120: inside literature range
  Coal avoided cost USD 63/t vs typical global USD 60-90/t
  Earlier Indian case studies (Energy Procedia 54) report +Rs 2.2-2.6/kWh and Rs 2600-3200/t avoided.
  Our Rs/kWh and Rs/t values are higher than those older figures. Some of the gap is
  probably cost escalation since then, but that study's inputs are not reproduced here,
  so treat the gap as unexplained and compare in USD terms (global range above).

F. KEY ASSUMPTIONS (edit in config.py)
  WACC 10%, life 25 yr, coal Rs 5000/t, GCV 3800 kcal/kg, USD/INR 88
  Capex island ref USD 560 M at 400 t/h, location factor 0.45, TCI factor 1.15
  Transport and storage of CO2 are NOT included (capture + compression only).
  Capex is scaled from literature reference costs, so it is the largest uncertainty;
  see the tornado charts. The absorber model is a short-cut with a lumped KGa, not
  the calibrated rate-based model.
